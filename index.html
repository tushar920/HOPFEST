02 — WHY HOP?

KIDS NEED MORE
THAN SCREEN TIME.

Between school, homework, tuition and screens, finding time to simply move and play can be harder than ever.

DID YOU KNOW?
60 MINUTES A DAY

Kids aged 5–17 should get an average of 60 minutes of physical activity every day.

TOO MUCH SITTING MATTERS

Higher sedentary time is associated with poorer physical fitness and cardiometabolic health in children.

MOVEMENT MATTERS

Regular physical activity supports children's physical, cognitive and mental wellbeing.

SO, LET'S MAKE TIME TO MOVE.

HOP Fest is created to give kids a reason to put the screens down, get moving and just play.
And give families a reason to spend more time together.

LESS SCREEN. MORE PLAY. MORE HOP.
